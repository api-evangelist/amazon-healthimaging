Across R&D organizations, AI agents are beginning to design molecules, plan syntheses, execute assays, and analyze experimental data. But these systems often operate without awareness of prior experiments, failed approaches, or parallel work happening across the organization. In this blog, learn how Sanofi recognized this limitation early and partnered with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to reimagine the foundation for AI-enabled discovery.
