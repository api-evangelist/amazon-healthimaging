In this blog, learn how Kite, a Gilead Company, modernized its bioinformatics infrastructure by transitioning from fragmented, project-specific workflows to a standardized, cloud-enabled approach using AWS HealthOmics. Discover how this collaboration with AWS Professional Services enabled over 100 researchers to access scalable, self-service computational workflows through their existing laboratory systems—without requiring CLI or infrastructure expertise.
